## 3. SEO Technical Directives Summary

* **Canonicalization:** Every page specifies self-referencing canonical tag `<link rel="canonical" href="https://plushcomfort.uk/..." />`.
* **Robots Directives:** Private and administrative views (`/admin`, `/checkout`, `/account`) are disallowed in `robots.txt` and flagged with `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, follow" />`.
* **Image Optimisation:** All furniture assets include descriptive `alt` tags including product name, upholstery material, and dimensions.
* **Pagination & Filters:** Category filter modifications use client state without creating duplicate shallow indexed URLs.

## 4. Content Management System (CMS) & Topical Authority Architecture

Plush Comfort UK features a dedicated, private Content Management System located at `/admin` (`Content CMS & SEO` tab) that allows store owners to create, publish, schedule, and organize SEO content without touching code.

### Supported Content Types:
1. **Flagship Pillar Pages (`pillar_page`):** Authoritative high-level buying guides (e.g. *The Ultimate UK Sofa Buying Guide*, *UK Bed Sizes & Storage Explained*). These act as topic cluster roots.
2. **Supporting Cluster Articles (`cluster_article`):** Focused sub-query articles answering specific user intent (e.g. *7 Easy Ways to Refresh Your Living Room*, *Small Bedroom Organisation Ideas*).
3. **How-To Guides (`howto_guide`):** Practical step-by-step masterclasses (e.g. *How to Measure a Room for a Sofa*, *How to Clean and Maintain a Luxury Fabric Sofa*).
4. **Comparison & Advice Articles (`comparison_article`):** Objective feature matrices (e.g. *Fabric vs Velvet & Leather*, *Ottoman vs Divan vs Standard Frames*).
5. **Buying Resources & FAQs (`faq_resource`):** Customer technical FAQs (e.g. *Buying Resources & FAQs for UK Bed Buyers*).
6. **General Buying Guides (`buying_guide`):** Seasonal or room-specific guides.
7. **Standard Blog Posts (`blog_post`):** Store announcements and Yorkshire craftsman updates.

### Bidirectional Linking Mechanism:
* **Pillar &rarr; Cluster:** Pillar pages dynamically render an **"In This Topic Cluster"** component displaying all nested cluster articles.
* **Cluster &rarr; Pillar:** Cluster articles automatically render a prominent **"Part of Pillar Topic Cluster"** banner linking directly to the parent pillar.
* **Cluster &rarr; Commercial Product:** Articles feature rich markdown links to products (`/product/:slug`) and categories (`/category/:slug`), plus a bottom **"Pieces Featured in this Guide"** product carousel.
* **Orphan Content Detection:** The CMS dashboard identifies any cluster articles lacking a parent pillar or containing 0 internal links.
* **Dynamic XML Sitemap:** Automatically generates and exports production `sitemap.xml` entries for all published, indexable articles while filtering out drafts and noindex pages.
